Career Path

Despite shining at Santos, he was overlooked. They said scoring goals in Brazil meant nothing. They said anyone could do it in that league. Then, when the world's biggest stage called, fate took away his chance to finish what he had started. They said Brazil would have lost anyway, even if he had been there. He moved to Barcelona, and they said he was living in Messi’s shadow. He moved to Paris, and they called it a mistake. Every time he got injured, they blamed him. They said he was wasting his talent, destroying his future, throwing away his prime. At every World Cup he played, fate seemed to find another way to break his heart. And when his body failed him, they gave him another name: Injury-prone. When he chased a different path, they called him money-hungry. They questioned his decisions, mocked his tears, criticized his lifestyle, and judged his failures as if they had never dreamed of anything themselves. But they forgot something. Neymar was never just a footballer chasing trophies. He was a boy who made the world fall in love with football through his magic. And when he returned to Santos after his time in Saudi Arabia, the story took another turn. He returned to the club where it all began, not to silence the critics with words, but to fight for the team that had given him his first chance. Santos were fighting to survive, and Neymar fought alongside them. He played through pain, carried the weight of expectations, and faced the impossible choice between saving his club and protecting his dream of one more World Cup. For a man who had already given so much to football, he had every reason to walk away. But he stayed and fought. Not because the world believed in him. But because he still believed in himself. Perhaps he will never win every trophy they expected him to win. Perhaps his career will always be remembered for the injuries, the what-ifs, and the dreams that slipped away. But tell me something: How do you measure a player who made millions stand up from their seats? Who turned defenders into spectators? Who made the impossible look effortless? Who made an entire generation fall in love with the beautiful game? How do you measure a career with trophies alone? The world kept asking Neymar to prove himself. He did it with every dribble, every comeback, every moment of magic. And even when fate refused to give him the ending he deserved, he kept writing his story. They may remember the trophies he didn't win. But we will remember the magic they could never take away. You are not defined only by what you achieve, but also by everything you gave to become who you are.

Achievements & Honors

Neymar’s Samba d’Or Wins
2014, 2015, 2017, 2020, 2021, 2022

awarded to the best Brazilian in Europe — six times, celebrating his skill, flair, and impact on the pitch.

Olympic Gold Medal
2016

Scored the winning penalty to secure Brazil's first Olympic football gold medal.

UEFA Champions League
2015

Won the Champions League with Barcelona as part of the legendary MSN trio.

FIFA Confederations Cup
2013

Won the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player, leading Brazil to victory.

Copa Libertadores
2011

Led Santos to their first Copa Libertadores title since Pelé's era, scoring in the final.

FIFA Puskás Award
2011

Neymar's Puskás Award (2011) was given by FIFA for the most beautiful goal of the year—an incredible solo goal against Flamengo, featuring multiple dribbles from midfield to finish.

National Team Records

Brazil’s all-time top scorer with 80 goals
